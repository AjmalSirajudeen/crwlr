# Product Context

## Target Users
- Primary: Young professionals (25-35)
- Secondary: College students (21-24)
- Tertiary: Venue owners and managers

## User Needs
1. Social Connection
   - Easy way to plan nights out with friends
   - Share experiences and discoveries
   - Meet like-minded people

2. Discovery
   - Find new venues
   - Get recommendations
   - Explore different areas

3. Coordination
   - Organize group outings
   - Track friends' locations
   - Manage schedules

## User Experience Goals
- Intuitive and engaging interface
- Seamless social integration
- Real-time updates and notifications
- Gamification for engagement
- Mobile-first design
